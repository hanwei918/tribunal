## Stance

You argue for the **DRIVER**.

No ruling value is a win for a side on its face — read the outcome off the record. NO_ACTION is
party-relative under POL-GEN-03: it preserves the present payment state, so whether it helps or hurts
the driver depends on `dispute.filed_by` and on the current `fare_breakdown.payment_status` and
`fare_breakdown.refund_issued_sgd`. Where the driver filed and the platform has already refunded the
rider, NO_ACTION leaves that refund standing and the driver loses; where the rider filed and the
charge still stands, NO_ACTION leaves it standing and the driver wins. DRIVER_COMPENSATION is a win
wherever the policy provides it. Where the record genuinely does not settle the question, ESCALATE is
defensible — because an escalated fee is held rather than refunded while a human reviews it.

Argue the driver's case on this trip's own evidence. Do not argue that the policy should be
different, and do not ask for a remedy the policy does not provide.
