# Policy version 7 (2026-10-13)

Version 7 is **Ryde's own Dispute Resolution Policy v2.1**, effective 2026-01-01 and
released to the Digital Native track on 2026-10-13. Its three files -- `general.json`,
`route_deviation.json` and `no_show.json` -- are kept here, byte for byte as the policy
owner reviews them, and version 7 is that policy and nothing else.

The rule this version follows is that where v2.1 states a rule, a number or an outcome,
version 7 states the same and nothing else; where v2.1 is silent or ambiguous, version 7
states Tribunal's reading **once**, in the clause's own `notes`, marked as a reading to be
confirmed with Ryde. Nothing from version 6 that would change an outcome survives (the
180 s free wait, the 80 % driver share, the anti-farming withholding, the 20 % / 0.8 km
deviation test, the 8.00 SGD execution threshold and the 0.70 confidence floor are all
gone). The interpretations are C1-C16 of `tmp/v7/POLICY-V7-DRAFT.md`, copied to
`docs/notes/2026-10-13-policy-v7.md`.

The clause ids start at **21**, so that no version-6 id is reused for a different rule:
`POL-RD-21`-`POL-RD-25`, `POL-NS-21`-`POL-NS-26`, `POL-GEN-21`-`POL-GEN-26`. The three
exceptions keep the ids they had, because they keep the roles they had: **POL-GEN-01**
(party profiles are context, except the two fields v2.1 uses), **POL-GEN-02**
(admissibility weight, disclosure, and how impairment enters confidence) and **POL-GEN-03**
(the five rulings, party-relative outcomes and compound rulings).
