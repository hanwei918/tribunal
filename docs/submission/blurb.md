# Candidate blurbs

Five one-line blurbs, each under ten words. The first is the policy owner's.
Every count is printed by

    printf '%s' "<blurb>" | tr '-' ' ' | wc -w

which counts a hyphenated compound as its parts; under that rule the policy
owner's own blurb is the seven words the owner gives it.

| # | blurb | words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence-cited AI arbitration for ride disputes | 7 |
| 2 | Two advocates argue one brief; a referee checks citations | 9 |
| 3 | Automated ride dispute rulings that cite their evidence | 8 |
| 4 | A support team's assistant for ride disputes | 7 |
| 5 | Appealable arbitration for Ryde ride and no-show disputes | 9 |

Line 1 is the policy owner's. Lines 2 to 5 are candidates drafted here, each
under ten words by the same count.
