# Candidate blurbs

Six one-line blurbs, each under ten words. Every count is printed by

    printf '%s' "<blurb>" | tr '-' ' ' | wc -w

which counts a hyphenated compound as its parts; under that rule the policy
owner's own blurb is the seven words the owner gives it.

| # | blurb | words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Multi-agent ride-dispute arbitration that cites its evidence** — **chosen** | 9 |
| 2 | Evidence-cited AI arbitration for ride disputes | 7 |
| 3 | Two advocates argue one brief; a referee checks citations | 9 |
| 4 | Automated ride dispute rulings that cite their evidence | 8 |
| 5 | A support team's assistant for ride disputes | 7 |
| 6 | Appealable arbitration for Ryde ride and no-show disputes | 9 |

**Candidate 1 is chosen**: it is the blurb the submission carries. It is the
candidate this part puts first, the one that says multi-agent:
**9 words and 60 characters**, both under the limits the brief sets (under ten
words, under eighty characters). Line 2 is the policy owner's. Lines 2 to 6 are
candidates drafted here, each under ten words by the same count.
