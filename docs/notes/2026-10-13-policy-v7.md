# Policy version 7: Ryde Dispute Resolution Policy v2.1, as Tribunal applies it (DRAFT 2026-10-13)

**Status.** Draft by the owner's side, for the policy owner (Ji Guofang) to review on

released to the Digital Native track on 2026-10-13, with the samples DISP-001,

**The rule of this version.** Where Ryde v2.1 states a rule, a number or an
outcome, version 7 states the same and nothing else. No version-6 rule that would
change an outcome survives (the 180 s free wait, the 80% driver share, the
anti-farming withholding, the 20% / 0.8 km deviation test, the 8.00 SGD execution
threshold and the 0.70 confidence floor are all gone). Version 7 adds only:

- **interpretations** (Part C) where v2.1 is silent or ambiguous, each stated
  once, each marked as Tribunal's reading, and each to be confirmed with Ryde;
- **procedure** (Part B) that never changes a v2.1 outcome: citation, evidence
  that cannot establish a fact v2.1 needs, masking, appeal, audit.

Clause ids: the files in `tmp/v7/policy/` are the version-7 text. `R7-...` below maps to them:
R7-RD-01..05 = POL-RD-21..25; R7-NS-01..06 = POL-NS-21..26; R7-GEN-01 = POL-GEN-21,

## Part A -- Ryde v2.1's rules

### Route deviation (v2.1 Section 1, Section 6.2)

**R7-RD-01 Notification (v2.1 1.1, 1.2, 1.4).** A driver may reroute from the
recommended route for real-time traffic, a road closure or congestion, but must
inform the rider of the deviation by in-app chat, or verbally with the trip call
recording as evidence, before or at the point of deviation; a brief reason is
enough. Where the chat log or a call recording confirms the rider's
acknowledgment, the deviation is agreed.

**R7-RD-02 Measuring the deviation (v2.1 1.4).** Deviation distance =
actual route distance - recommended route distance, in km.

**R7-RD-03 Traffic justification (v2.1 1.4).** A reroute is traffic-justified
only where traffic data for the recommended route segment at the trip time shows
congestion, an incident or a closure.

**R7-RD-04 Refund matrix (v2.1 1.3).** Exactly one row applies:

| Traffic-justified | Rider informed | Deviation | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| yes | yes, and agreed | > 1 km | no refund |
| yes | no | > 1 km | partial refund of the excess distance fare |
| yes | no | <= 1 km | no refund (within tolerance) |
| no | (not relevant) | > 1 km | full refund of the excess distance fare |
| no | (not relevant) | <= 1 km | no refund (within tolerance) |

**R7-RD-05 Who decides a route deviation (v2.1 6.2).** The AI ruling stands only
where confidence >= 0.80 **and** the deviation is <= 2 km; otherwise the case is
escalated. *(See C1: this conflicts with DISP-001.)*

### No-show and cancellation (v2.1 Section 2, Section 6.2)

**R7-NS-01 Free wait (v2.1 2.1).** The driver waits 5 minutes of free wait time
from the scheduled pickup time; after it, the driver may cancel as a rider
no-show. *(See C2 for on-demand trips.)*

**R7-NS-02 The fee (v2.1 2.2).** The no-show cancellation fee is 5.00 SGD,
charged to the rider when the driver cancels after the free wait, and
transferred to the driver. *(See C5 for a record that charged another amount.)*

**R7-NS-03 Contact obligation (v2.1 2.3).** Before cancelling for no-show the
driver must have sent at least one in-app chat message **and** placed at least
one in-app call to the rider. If either is missing, the fee is voided and the
rider is not charged.

**R7-NS-04 Driver no-show (v2.1 2.4).** If the driver fails to arrive at the
pickup location by the scheduled pickup time + 5 minutes, the rider may cancel at
no charge. If the driver also failed to attempt contact, the rider is due a
5.00 SGD goodwill credit.

**R7-NS-05 Repeat no-show flag (v2.1 2.5).** A rider with 3 or more no-show
cancellations within a 30-day rolling window is flagged for review (upfront
payment, temporary suspension pending Trust & Safety review). This is a referral;
it does not change the ruling on the trip. *(See C7.)*

**R7-NS-06 Who decides a no-show (v2.1 6.2).** The AI ruling is final (the fee is
fixed at 5.00 SGD), subject to R7-GEN-01 and R7-GEN-02.

### Safety (v2.1 Section 5)

**R7-GEN-01 Safety is always human (v2.1 5.1, 5.2, 6.2).** A dispute flagged as
safety-related, or whose record carries a safety indicator (reckless or dangerous
driving, harassment, assault or threat of assault, inappropriate conduct, a
traffic accident, a party feeling unsafe), is escalated whatever its category.
The AI may gather evidence and prepare the case file; it may not rule, find
fault or recommend a resolution on it. Interim measures (5.3) are the human
reviewer's.

### Thresholds (v2.1 Section 6)

**R7-GEN-02 Value and confidence (v2.1 6.1, 6.3).** Dispute value < 10 SGD: the
AI ruling is final at any confidence. 10 to 50 SGD: the AI ruling stands only at
confidence >= 0.80, else escalate. > 50 SGD: always human review; the AI prepares
the case file only. Where any party carries a fraud flag of 1 or more, the
confidence required is 0.95 whatever the value. The category rules R7-RD-05 and
R7-NS-06 apply on top. *(See C4, C6.)*

### Precedents (v2.1 Section 7)

**R7-GEN-03 Precedents (v2.1 7).** Past rulings on similar cases are referenced
for consistency, searched by dispute type and key facts, and cited. v2.1's three
precedents (7.1 DISP-2026-0089, 7.2 DISP-2026-0142, 7.3 DISP-2026-0211) are
entered in the precedent store as version-7 precedents.

### Out of scope for the AI (v2.1 Sections 3 and 4)

**R7-GEN-04 Fare and property-damage disputes.** Fare and surge disputes (v2.1
Section 3) and property-damage claims (v2.1 Section 4) are not adjudicated by the
AI in this version; they are routed to human review with the v2.1 section that
governs them named in the packet. *(Scope decision, not a v2.1 rule: the track
asks for two categories; Section 4 needs image verification.)*

## Part B -- Tribunal procedure (changes no v2.1 outcome)

**R7-PROC-01 Cite or it does not count.** Every claim and every ruling cites the
evidence id it rests on and the clause (with its v2.1 section). v2.1 ends: "All
AI agents must cite the applicable policy section in their ruling rationale."

**R7-PROC-02 A fact the record cannot establish.** Where a fact a v2.1 rule needs
(the deviation distance, whether the driver notified, whether the driver called,
when the driver arrived) cannot be established because the evidence for it is
missing or impaired (a GPS gap, a truncated chat log, conflicting sources), the
case is escalated with the open question stated. This is the conservative reading
of v2.1 6.3, which counts evidence completeness in confidence; it is not a new
outcome.

**R7-PROC-03 Masking, appeal, audit.** Telephone numbers, e-mail addresses and
vehicle plates are masked before a model reads them and before display; an AI
ruling may be appealed once to a different human reviewer; every call is
logged. None of these changes a ruling.

## Part C -- Interpretations (Tribunal's readings; to confirm with Ryde)

- **C1 Route deviation over 2 km.** v2.1 6.2 escalates any deviation > 2 km, but
  DISP-001 (4.3 km) expects an AI partial refund of 3.44. *Proposed:* follow the
  sample (AI rules), record the conflict, and switch to 6.2 if Ryde says so.
  Asked in the track group 2026-10-13.
- **C2 Free wait start.** v2.1 2.1 says "from the scheduled pickup time"; DISP-002
  starts the timer at the driver's arrival (08:43, ahead of the 08:45 schedule).
  *Proposed:* from `trip_record.scheduled_at` where the trip is scheduled **and**
  the driver arrived before it; otherwise from the driver's arrival. Asked.
- **C3 Excess distance fare.** = deviation km x `fare_breakdown.per_km_rate_sgd`,
  without surge (DISP-001: 4.3 x 0.80 = 3.44). *Proposed:* no surge. Asked.
- **C4 Dispute value.** *Proposed:* `dispute.amount_contested_sgd` (the amount the
  filer asks back), not the trip fare. DISP-001: 6.50 -> < 10 SGD band.
- **C5 Partial vs full refund of the excess distance fare (1.3).** DISP-001 refunds
  the whole excess distance fare (3.44) under the row v2.1 calls "partial".
  *Proposed:* both rows (traffic-justified and not informed; not justified) pay the
  whole excess distance fare; "partial" describes the trip fare (only part of it
  comes back) and "full" the excess distance fare. Ruling label PARTIAL_REFUND in
  both. Asked.
- **C6 Fraud flags.** Our case schema carries no fraud flag; Ryde's profiles carry
  `fraud_flags`. *Proposed:* add an optional `fraud_flags` field to both
  profiles (default 0 when absent); 6.3's 0.95 bar applies only when it is >= 1.
- **C7 Repeat no-show counter.** Our profiles count no-show **disputes** in 30
  days, not no-show **cancellations**. *Proposed:* add an optional
  `no_show_cancellations_30d` to the rider profile; R7-NS-05 applies only when it
  is present.
- **C8 Fee on the record.** Some records charge a fee other than 5.00. *Proposed:*
  the ruling acts on the fee charged and adds a referral when it differs from
  v2.1's 5.00.
- **C9 Rulings vocabulary.** v2.1 "UPHELD" (fee stands, goes to the driver) ->
  DRIVER_COMPENSATION with both amount fields = the fee; "voided" -> FULL_REFUND
  of the fee; driver no-show -> FULL_REFUND of anything charged, plus the 5.00
  goodwill as a referral; route deviation refunds -> PARTIAL_REFUND; no refund ->
  NO_ACTION; anything a rule sends to a person -> ESCALATE.
- **C10 "Confidence".** The judge's stated confidence, already lowered by the
  admissibility pre-pass's weights (v2.1 6.3: evidence completeness, policy
  clarity, precedent alignment, credibility).
- **C11 Verbal notification.** Our records hold no call recordings. *Proposed:* a
  verbal notice cannot be established from our records; a driver's claim of one,
  with an in-app call placed before the deviation, is escalated under
  R7-PROC-02 rather than accepted or rejected.

- **C12 Rounding.** v2.1 is silent. *Proposed:* to the cent, half up (DISP-001 is
  exact).
- **C13 Driver no-show deadline on an on-demand trip.** v2.1 2.4 uses "scheduled
  pickup time + 5 minutes". *Proposed:* where the record carries no scheduled time,
  POL-GEN-26 applies (escalate) unless the driver plainly arrived (then 2.4 does not
  arise).
- **C14 Repeat no-show count.** *Proposed:* counts this trip when its fee stands.
- **C15 Fraud flag on a no-show.** 6.2 says a no-show AI ruling is final; 6.3 says a
  fraud flag raises the bar to 0.95 "regardless of dispute value". *Proposed:* 6.3
  applies to no-shows too (DISP-002's rider carries a fraud flag).
- **C17 Which distance.** *Proposed:* the trip record's actual and recommended distances
  (DISP-001: 24.8 - 20.5 = 4.3 km). DISP-001's 16-fix GPS trace measures 20.75 km and would
  put the deviation at 0.85 km, inside the tolerance; a sparse trace understates length.
- **C16 Arrival test.** v2.1 does not say how arrival is established. *Proposed:*
  the trip record's arrival event corroborated by the GPS trace within the zone's
  arrival radius (version 6's radii, as an evidence procedure only).

## Amendments (2026-10-13 evening)

**C18 -- the kinds of traffic event, as a map.** POL-RD-23 names its kinds in
words -- "congestion, an incident or a closure" -- and the record names its
advisories in tokens, so the two could only be matched by folding a word against
a token, which is a judgement rather than a reading. `route_deviation.json` now
carries the mapping as a structured field,
`thresholds.traffic_justification_types`: `congestion` is `CONGESTION` and
`HEAVY_CONGESTION`, `incident` is `ACCIDENT`, `VEHICLE_FIRE`, `BREAKDOWN` and
`INCIDENT`, and `closure` is `CLOSURE` and `ROADWORKS`. An accident is therefore
an incident because the policy says so, and a type the map does not carry -- a
kerb restriction, a traffic flow -- justifies no reroute however close its word
looks. The field's own `note` says which two it names as not justifying.

**C16 amended -- the accuracy filter reaches only a fix that reports an
accuracy.** C16's arrival test corroborated the trip record's arrival marker
with a GPS fix inside the zone's arrival radius whose reported accuracy passed
the policy's filter. `no_show.json`'s `arrival_test` now says
`accuracy_filter_applies`: "only to a fix that reports an accuracy; a fix with
no reported accuracy is used at its reported position". A fix that reports none
is of unknown accuracy, not an inaccurate one, and it is used as it stands; the
brief says so beside the reading. This is what DISP-002 turns on: its only fix
within the window sits at the pickup point and reports no accuracy, so the
arrival is corroborated.
