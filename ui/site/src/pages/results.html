These are the cases it was built on, so they're not a fair test. Every miss is explained in the repo.

Recorded runs on synthetic cases: 'executed' means the system would have executed the ruling; no money moved. 'Confidence' is the judge's own figure, capped by the evidence weights, not a measured accuracy.

No test set came from outside the team. The corpus was written by the team; dev-v2 and dev-v2-ext by Ji Guofang; wild-v2 by OpenAI Codex for the owner; sealed-v1 by Claude; sealed-v2 by the policy owner with OpenAI Codex. The sealed sets were never developed against, but they are not independent benchmarks.

sealed-v1: the policy owner's AI assistant opened its cases and key before the freeze; the key was re-derived at version 6 before the run. sealed-v2: made by the policy owner with OpenAI Codex; its manifest's case-by-case sign-off before the run was not met and is pending.
