Version 6

The team's own policy, frozen on 2026-10-11 and kept as history. The older case pages and the queue show its runs.

  • 24-72 h Human review, today. How long a person takes today, per the challenge.
  • 160.4 s Median per case. 9 in 10 cases done within 4.8 minutes. The AI calls run side by side; an earlier one-at-a-time run averaged 429.6 s (a different set, so not a strict comparison).
  • $0.0579 Model cost, median. Typical AI cost per case.
  • 48 h Case closed within, the team's target. The team's own target, not Ryde's. The queue flags any case that runs past it.
  • 112 Decided and executed. Under the SGD 8.00 threshold.
  • 13 Decided, money held. Above SGD 8.00, a person releases it.
  • 26 No ruling issued. Safety 2, failed checks 3, split decisions 4, judge escalated under the policy 17.

What the code allows today, and what it would cost. Worked out from the code and the saved runs; not a load test.

  • 6 AI calls per case. Six AI calls per case by design: two lawyers and four judge drafts (a draft that fails its check is retried once). Everything else is plain code.
  • ~4-6 Cases a minute per model, the ceiling. The token limit binds first: about 4.4 a minute if cached tokens count against it, about 6 if not. The 60-request limit (10 a minute) does not bind. Computed, not measured.
  • ~$68 Per 1,000 cases. Model cost at the mean, 10.33 USD over the 151 version-6 cases ($58 at the 0.0579 USD median).

Queue targets the team set (ops/config.json, not Ryde's): automatic ruling 5 min, human review 24 h, safety 2 h, case closed 48 h.

Test cases written separately and hidden from the system. Each set was run once.

  • 12/12 sealed-v2, 12 cases. 12 of 12 agreed with the key.   • 21/24 sealed-v1, 24 cases. 20/24 against the answers as first written.

  • 0 rulings flipped by an injection. 6 attacks: 5 had no effect, 1 was sent to a person.
  • 136/141 agreed when the sides were swapped. Same result after swapping the two sides.
  • 0 misses toward the rider. Of 8 mistakes, none favoured the rider; 5 just sent the case to a person.
  • 0.088 expected calibration error. How well its confidence matches reality (lower is better).
