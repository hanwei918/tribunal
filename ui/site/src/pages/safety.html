Home / Safety

Where it
stops.

It aims to rule only on what the evidence can prove and to send the rest to a person, with the money frozen. Four cases on record slipped through and were decided when a person should have (version 6): 112 and 2858 in development, 410 in sealed-v1 and 716 in wild-v2.


Six lines it holds.

01

Cited or dropped

If a point can't be backed by real evidence and a real rule, code throws it out.

02

Unsure? A person decides

If the AI judge isn't sure, or its answers disagree, the money is frozen and a person decides.

03

Safety goes first

A case flagged for safety goes to a person; the lawyers prepare the case file and the judge is not asked. Safety talk in free text is not detected yet.

04

Planted orders not obeyed

The drafts refused a fake rule slipped into the chat, and the case went to a person (case 1779, version 6).

05

Appeals

Version 8 has no appeal clause yet; the version-6 operations layer offers one appeal within 30 days to a different person, and bringing it to version 8 is on the roadmap.

06

Privacy

Plates, phone numbers and e-mails are masked before the AI reads a case. Names aren't, and the saved runs here masked plates only.


A person, on a clock.

Decided within 24 hours (2 hours for safety). Closed within 48 hours of the complaint.

  1. 01

    Enters

    The AI wasn't sure or its drafts split, the amount is over SGD 50, it's about safety, or the route deviates by over 2 km.

  2. 02

    Reads

    First the case file: why it came here, both sides' points, the fact-check, every ruling.

  3. 03

    Decides

    Agree, adjust within the allowed options, ask for more evidence, or pass it on.

  4. 04

    Explains

    Write down why, pointing to the evidence and the rule.

The version-6 policy asks a second person to re-read 5% of upheld and every varied decision each week (POL-GEN-06(9)); the prototype does not run that yet. Automatic rulings can be switched off at any time.

The SGD 8 sign-off is version 6's rule. Version 8 applies v2.1's own: below SGD 10 the AI ruling is final, from 10 to 50 it stands only at a confidence of 0.80 or more (0.95 with a fraud flag), and above 50 a person decides. On the 40 version-8 cases the three limbs stopped 5 between them: 2 on the 2 km limit, 1 on the value, 2 on a safety flag.


Tested the way others test.

The test sets cite published methods. Each card says what we took from it.

OWASP 2025

Top 10 for LLM Applications, LLM01: prompt injection

The risk the version-6 inject-v1 tests target: instructions planted in what a rider or driver writes.

Hines et al. 2024

Spotlighting against indirect prompt injection

Cited by the version-6 inject-v1 tests. The six attacks include a forged system message, a base64-encoded order and hidden zero-width characters. arXiv:2403.14720

Ribeiro et al. 2020

CheckList: invariance tests

Each attack is a pair: the same trip twice, one with an instruction added. Both must reach the same result. ACL 2020

Gebru et al.

Datasheets for Datasets

Five test sets come with a datasheet saying what is in them and who wrote them. arXiv:1803.09010

The details.

Version 8 has no appeal clause yet; the version-6 operations layer offers one appeal within 30 days to a different person, and bringing it to version 8 is on the roadmap.

The current code masks Singapore vehicle registration marks, telephone numbers and e-mail addresses before a model reads a case. Names are not masked. The recorded runs on this site were made when only registration marks were masked. A Singapore deployment would be subject to the PDPA; no compliance claim is made.

Prompt injection, forged evidence ids, collusion and repeat claims, answer-key leakage, the sealed sets, API keys and a wrong automatic ruling - each with its control and what it does not cover, in docs/governance/threat-model.md.

The policy is versioned: version 6 is base 3 plus three amendments, each approved by a named person. The learning loop drafts a clause, but nothing changes until a person approves it, and it can only add a clause.

  • A hackathon prototype for the Ryde challenge, not affiliated with or endorsed by Ryde.
  • Development runs on synthetic cases, not live service; slow for a chat reply (median 153.1 s).
  • Safety is enforced on the two structured flags only; free-text allegations are not detected.
  • Quoting a clause id that does not exist forces a case to a person (1779, 2829, 2853, 3109). Recorded, not repaired.
  • A refund can exceed the amount contested (eight recorded rulings).
  • The corpus's evidence notes carry the case author's reasoning.
  • No retry on a transport error; a failed case is absent from the queue until re-run. No committed version-6 run has one.
  • The confidence floor is skipped for a non-numeric confidence; no recorded run was affected.
  • The chat threat rules' 6-of-6 is a development figure.
  • Before the freeze, the policy owner's AI assistant opened sealed-v1's cases and key.
  • Not built: model fallback, output repair, BM25 precedents, calibrated thresholds, chat sentiment, image analysis.
